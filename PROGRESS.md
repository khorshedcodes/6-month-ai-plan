### Useful Standup & PR Templates
- **Daily Standup:**
  - *Yesterday:* "Yesterday, I implemented..."
  - *Today:* "Today, I plan to work on..."
  - *Blockers:* "I'm currently blocked by... / No blockers at the moment."
- **Explaining a Technical Choice:**
  - "I chose X over Y because it gives us..."
  - "The trade-off here is between [readability/memory/speed] and..."
- **Asking for Help Like a Senior Dev:**
  - "I'm trying to achieve X. I expected Y, but instead I'm getting Z. So far, I've checked A and B."
