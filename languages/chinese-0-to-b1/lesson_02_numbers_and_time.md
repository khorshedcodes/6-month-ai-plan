# Lesson 2: Numbers & Time Reference Card

## 1. Numbers 1 to 10 in Mandarin

| Number | Hanzi (Character) | Pinyin | Tone | Pronunciation Anchor |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **一** | *yī* | 1st (high flat) | "Yee" (sing it high) |
| **2** | **二** | *èr* | 4th (sharp falling) | "Arr" (sharp drop) |
| **3** | **三** | *sān* | 1st (high flat) | "Sahn" |
| **4** | **四** | *sì* | 4th (sharp falling) | "Suh" |
| **5** | **五** | *wǔ* | 3rd (dipping low) | "Woo" (deep dip) |
| **6** | **六** | *liù* | 4th (sharp falling) | "Leo" |
| **7** | **七** | *qī* | 1st (high flat) | "Chee" (high pitch) |
| **8** | **八** | *bā* | 1st (high flat) | "Bah" |
| **9** | **九** | *jiǔ* | 3rd (dipping low) | "Jyo" (dipping) |
| **10** | **十** | *shí* | 2nd (rising) | "Shir?" (like a question) |

---

## 2. Expressing Time (Hours)
- **点** (*diǎn*) = o'clock / hour
- **7 o'clock:** **七点** (*qī diǎn*)
- **3 o'clock:** **三点** (*sān diǎn*)

---

## 3. Workplace Sentence Pattern:
- **上班** (*shàngbān*) = to go to work / start shift
- **我七点上班。** (*Wǒ qī diǎn shàngbān.*) = "I start work at 7 o'clock."
