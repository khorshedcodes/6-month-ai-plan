# Lesson 5: Days of the Week & Weekly Schedule

## 1. Days of the Week

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **星期一** | *xīngqīyī* | Monday | Week 1 |
| **星期二** | *xīngqī'èr* | Tuesday | Week 2 |
| **星期三** | *xīngqīsān* | Wednesday | Week 3 |
| **星期四** | *xīngqīsì* | Thursday | Week 4 |
| **星期五** | *xīngqīwǔ* | Friday | Week 5 (Uni Day!) |
| **星期六** | *xīngqīliù* | Saturday | Week 6 |
| **星期天** | *xīngqītiān* | Sunday | Week Sun |

---

## 2. Useful Time Phrases
- **今天** (*jīntiān*) = Today
- **明天** (*míngtiān*) = Tomorrow
- **昨天** (*zuótiān*) = Yesterday

### Practice Sentences:
- **今天星期一。** (*Jīntiān xīngqīyī.*) = "Today is Monday."
- **今天星期天。** (*Jīntiān xīngqītiān.*) = "Today is Sunday."
- **今天星期几？** (*Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?*) = "What day is today?"
