# Pinyin and Pronunciation Foundation Guide

## The 4 Tones of Mandarin Chinese
Mandarin has 4 tones + 1 neutral tone. Tone changes the meaning of a word completely!

| Tone | Name | Pitch Contour | Description | Example | Meaning |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st** | High Flat | 5-5 (High & steady) | Like singing a steady high note: *ā* | **mā** (妈) | Mother |
| **2nd** | Rising | 3-5 (Low to high) | Like asking a question: *"Huh?"*: *á* | **má** (麻) | Hemp / Numb |
| **3rd** | Dipping | 2-1-4 (Falls then rises) | Low, deep dip: *ǎ* | **mǎ** (马) | Horse |
| **4th** | Falling | 5-1 (High to sharp drop) | Sharp and decisive, like saying *"No!"*: *à* | **mà** (骂) | Scold |
| **Neutral** | Light | Short & soft | No tone mark | **ma** (吗) | Question particle |

## Lesson 1: Essential First Words & Tech Greetings

| Hanzi (Characters) | Pinyin | Meaning | Usage / Audio Mental Guide |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **你好** | *Nǐ hǎo* | Hello | Standard greeting (literally "You good") |
| **我** | *Wǒ* | I / Me | 3rd tone dipping |
| **是** | *Shì* | Am / Is / Are (To be) | 4th tone sharp drop |
| **工程师** | *Gōngchéngshī* | Engineer | *gōng* (1st), *chéng* (2nd), *shī* (1st) |
| **谢谢** | *Xièxie* | Thank you | 4th tone + neutral |
| **再见** | *Zàijiàn* | Goodbye | Literally "Again see" |

## Sentence Construction Formula 1:
**Subject + 是 (shì) + Noun / Profession**

- **我是工程师。** (*Wǒ shì gōngchéngshī.*) = "I am an engineer."
- **我是程序员。** (*Wǒ shì chéngxùyuán.*) = "I am a programmer."
