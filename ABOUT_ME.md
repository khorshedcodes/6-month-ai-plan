## 3. Work & University Schedule (Rotating 7-to-7 Shifts)

### Pattern A: Night Shift Week (7:00 PM – 7:00 AM)
- **Best Study Window:** During quiet night hours when mind is sharp and alert.
- **Strategy:** Use Night Shift weeks for **deep coding & new concepts** (90–120 min sessions, or split into two 45-min blocks).

### Pattern B: Day Shift Week (7:00 AM – 7:00 PM)
- **Best Study Windows:**
  - **Window 1:** 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM (focused morning window)
  - **Window 2:** After 4:00 PM
- **Strategy:** Split the daily routine into **two short blocks** if needed:
  - *Block 1 (30–40 min):* Learn + Implement (Steps 1–2)
  - *Block 2 (20–30 min):* Review + Quiz + Dev English (Steps 3–5)

### Fridays (University Day — Exhaustion Danger Zone)
- **Reality:** Attends university on Fridays. Especially after a Thursday Night Shift → Friday all-day University, **Friday night is extreme exhaustion**.
- **Rule:** **FRIDAY IS OFF / ZERO-PRESSURE DAY.**
  - Our "Week" has **6 active learning days + 1 Rest Day (Friday)**.
  - Never schedule heavy coding or tests on Friday night.

### CSE University Study / Exam Nights
- **Reality:** Some nights must go to CSE coursework or university exams.
- **Rule:** When university study happens, trigger **"Exam/Uni Mode"**:
  - Pause the 6-month clock with zero guilt, OR do a **5-minute English Standup** about what you studied for university (so you still practice English while studying CSE!).
