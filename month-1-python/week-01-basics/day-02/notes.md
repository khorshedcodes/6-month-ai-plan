# Day 2: Notes & Developer English Practice

## Topic: Control Flow, Loops, and Standup Updates

### 1. Key Concept Mastered
- **`for ... else` construct:** In Python, an `else` block directly attached to a `for` (or `while`) loop executes only if the loop completes all iterations without hitting a `break` statement.
- **Range with step:** `range(start, stop, step)` generates values up to (but not including) `stop` incremented by `step`.

## 3. English Corrections & Patterns to Remember
1. **Past tense consistency:**
   - Say: *"I learned and practiced..."* (simple past) instead of *"I have learn and prectice"*.
   - Say: *"I got familiar with..."* instead of *"I have get familer"*.
2. **Describing past blockers professionally:**
   - In standup, if you solved a problem yourself, frame it as a resolved issue: *"I was briefly stuck on X, but I resolved it after reviewing the docs."*
   - The phrase *"No blockers at the moment"* is the standard way to say you are ready to continue.
