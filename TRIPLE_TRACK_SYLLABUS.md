# Integrated 6-Month Master Plan: AI Engineering + English (B1→C1) + Chinese (0→B1/HSK3)

> **Philosophy:** High-leverage consistency over burnout. Every study day consists of **3 distinct, focused practices**:
> 1. **Coding Practice (60–75 min):** AI Engineering & Python Foundations (closed-book, no AI generation).
> 2. **Professional English Practice (25–30 min):** Elevating from **B1 (Intermediate) to C1 (Advanced / Professional Fluency)** for tech jobs, client communication, and international interviews.
> 3. **Chinese Practice (20–25 min):** Building from **Zero to B1 / HSK 3** (Pinyin, high-frequency characters, speaking drills, and tech terminology).

---

## 2. Language Track Roadmaps (Months 1–6)

### Track 2: English Elevation (CEFR B1 $\rightarrow$ C1)
- **Month 1 (B1 $\rightarrow$ B2 Transition):** Eliminating common non-native grammatical mistakes (tense consistency, articles, prepositions), eliminating passive hesitation, mastering structured workplace formats (standups, bug reports, PRs).
- **Month 2 (B2 Solidification):** Expanding precise technical vocabulary (trade-offs, latency, throughput, edge cases), describing data patterns and analytical reports without simple words like "good" or "bad".
- **Month 3 (B2 $\rightarrow$ C1 Transition):** The STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral tech interviews; practicing conflict resolution, justifying architectural trade-offs under pushback.
- **Month 4 (C1 Fluency - System Design & Deep Tech):** Explaining mathematical & deep learning concepts clearly to non-technical stakeholders vs senior engineering peers.
- **Month 5 (C1 Fluency - High-Level Negotiation):** Defending RAG architectures, discussing token economics, latency vs quality trade-offs, handling client requirements.
- **Month 6 (C1 Mastery - Job Offer & Interview Readiness):** Full 45-minute mock technical and behavioral interviews, salary negotiation scripts, executive summaries.

---

### Track 3: Chinese Mandarin (HSK 1 $\rightarrow$ HSK 3 / CEFR B1)
- **Month 1: The Foundations (HSK 1 Part 1 - Pinyin & Survival Basics)**
  - Tones (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, neutral), initials, finals, tone changes (半三声, 不, 一).
  - Basic greetings, numbers, self-introduction, nationality, job titles (工程师, 程序员).
  - Target: 60 basic vocabulary words + recognizing 30 basic Hanzi (汉字).
- **Month 2: Essential Daily Life (HSK 1 Complete - ~150 Words)**
  - Asking questions (`什么`, `谁`, `哪儿`, `几`, `吗`), time, dates, daily schedule (`今天`, `明天`, `点`).
  - Expressing wants and abilities (`想`, `要`, `会`, `可以`).
  - Target: Passing HSK 1 level mock check.
- **Month 3: Expanding Vocabulary & Action Verbs (HSK 2 Part 1 - ~220 Words)**
  - Describing actions in progress (`在...`), completed actions (`了`), past experiences (`过`).
  - Directions, locations, shopping, simple workplace communications.
- **Month 4: Daily Conversational Fluency (HSK 2 Complete - ~300 Words)**
  - Comparisons (`比`), expressing causation (`因为...所以...`), conditions (`虽然...但是...`).
  - Basic tech conversation: discussing computers, software, studying, and shift schedules.
  - Target: Passing HSK 2 level mock check.
- **Month 5: Stepping into Intermediate (HSK 3 Part 1 - ~450 Words)**
  - Compound sentences, directional complements (`出来`, `过去`), expressing opinion (`觉得`, `认为`).
  - Discussing engineering work, maintenance tasks, problem solving in Chinese.
- **Month 6: Functional B1 Conversationalist (HSK 3 Complete - ~600 Words)**
  - Connecting ideas smoothly (`不但...而且...`, `如果...就...`).
  - Handling travel, phone calls, software project overviews, and casual conversations with Chinese engineers.
  - Target: Passing HSK 3 / B1 level test.

## 3. How We Structure Each Day (The 3-Practice System)

Starting with **Day 18**, every daily session has 3 clear milestones:

```text
Today's 3-Part Session:
[Part 1: Coding (AI Engineering)]
- Topic: Python Packaging, src/ layout, ruff linting
- Hands-on task to code independently

[Part 2: Developer English (B1 -> C1)]
- Focus: Nuanced vocabulary & professional communication
- Today's task: Contrast words (However, In contrast, Whereas) + PR review comment

[Part 3: Chinese Mandarin (0 -> B1)]
- Focus: Pinyin initials/finals, 4 tones, 5 vocabulary words + 2 sentences
- Interactive drill: Tone pronunciation check & Pinyin reading
```
